# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for features and releases. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria before entering production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for projects
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Validate acceptance criteria with Product Manager and developers
- Identify quality gaps and blockers
- Coordinate with developers on test automation and CI integration
- Conduct manual QA and sign-off on feature acceptance
- Report quality metrics and defect trends

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce defect escape rate and production incidents
- Build confidence in release quality
- Drive test automation and continuous quality improvement

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Collaborates with Developers** on test automation strategies and code quality standards
- **Partners with Product Managers** to validate and refine acceptance criteria
- **Works with Project Managers** to plan QA timelines and identify quality risks
- **Supports Engineering Managers** with quality metrics for team performance

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and standups
- Test plan reviews and acceptance sign-off
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- CI/CD pipeline reviews and test automation discussions

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects own technical direction, design decisions, and architectural consistency across projects. They provide guidance on technology choices, scalability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and propose solutions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure code quality, scalability, and maintainability standards
- Mentor developers and guide implementation approach
- Evaluate technology choices and dependencies
- Ensure compliance with architectural standards
- Document technical decisions via Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Improve team technical capability and knowledge sharing
- Establish and enforce architectural consistency

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Mentors Developers** on best practices and technical standards
- **Advises Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Collaborates with Project Managers** on technical risk management and dependency planning
- **Partners with QA/Testing Leads** on test architecture and test automation strategies
- **Supports Engineering Managers** with technical capacity planning and team growth

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and technical specifications
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review feedback and technical guidance
- Risk register contribution for technical risks
- Technical mentoring and knowledge-sharing sessions

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors represent business interests, provide approval authority, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy. They advocate for project value and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and constraints
- Approve project scope, timeline, and budget
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Escalate issues impacting business objectives
- Support team when obstacles arise
- Communicate project value to leadership
- Ensure project outcomes align with business goals

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across the organization
- Remove organizational and resource blockers
- Enable successful project outcomes and stakeholder satisfaction

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Sets priorities** for Product Managers and Project Managers
- **Approves scope and budget** with input from Project Managers
- **Escalates blockers** for the entire project team
- **Provides business context** to help all roles understand project value
- **Attends key milestones** (kickoff, reviews, demos, retrospectives)

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval gates
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Escalation and decision requests
- Executive briefings on status and outcomes
- Risk and dependency escalation

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers manage engineering teams, own capacity planning, career development, and team escalations. They balance project delivery with team growth and wellbeing.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity across projects
- Conduct 1-on-1s and career development discussions
- Identify and resolve team blockers
- Escalate personnel or organizational issues
- Set team technical standards and practices
- Support hiring, onboarding, and team retention
- Advocate for team needs to leadership

### Goals
- Build high-performing, engaged teams
- Balance project delivery with team growth
- Reduce turnover and improve retention
- Develop technical talent and leadership pipeline

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Manages Developers** for career growth, workload, and team health
- **Partners with Project Managers** on resource allocation and capacity planning
- **Collaborates with Technical Leads** on technical standards and team capability building
- **Supports Product Managers** with technical team insights on feasibility and effort
- **Works with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on organizational escalations and resource needs

### Typical Communication
- 1-on-1s and team retrospectives
- Capacity planning and resource requests
- Escalations and organizational decisions
- Team retrospective action items
- Career development and performance discussions

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure security controls, compliance requirements, and risk mitigation in project delivery. They integrate security and regulatory considerations throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review threat models and security architecture
- Conduct security scanning and vulnerability assessment
- Ensure regulatory compliance (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2)
- Advise on incident response and data protection
- Track security risks in the risk register
- Provide security training and guidance to teams

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Protect customer and organizational data
- Enable compliant, secure releases
- Build security awareness across teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- **Advises Developers** on secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- **Works with Technical Leads/Architects** on security architecture and threat modeling
- **Collaborates with QA/Testing Leads** on security testing strategies
- **Informs Project Managers** on security risks and compliance timelines
- **Partners with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on compliance and regulatory requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews and threat modeling
- Security scanning results and remediation plans
- Incident response and blameless retrospectives
- Compliance audits and evidence collection
- Security training and awareness communications
